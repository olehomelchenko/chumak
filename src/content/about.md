# Syto

**Data wrangling in the browser** — clean, transform, and prepare your data without installing anything.

Syto is a browser-based tool for working with tabular data. Think Power Query, but runs anywhere — no installation, no accounts, no data leaving your machine.

## How it's made

Built by [Oleh Omelchenko](https://www.linkedin.com/in/olehomelchenko/) with [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/claude-code) doing most of the heavy lifting — expect solid data transformations and some rough edges on the UI.
